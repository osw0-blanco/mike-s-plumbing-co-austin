About Mike's Plumbing Co

Providing reliable pipes and honest service to Austin families since 2008.

A Local Founder with a Simple Goal

Mike started this company out of the back of his truck 15 years ago. After working for big corporate plumbing companies, he realized that Austin homeowners were tired of hidden fees and technicians who arrived late. He wanted to build something different.

He decided to focus on three things: showing up when promised, giving a fair price before starting the work, and cleaning up after the job is done. This "customer-first" approach helped us grow from a one-man shop to a full team of licensed experts.

Today, we are proud to be a staple in the Austin community. We treat every home like it's our own, whether we're fixing a small leak or replacing an entire water heater system.
